## The headline: faction interference was real, and it was one flaw in MilContext

DrMuck's hypothesis — "the faction RTS AIs are not clearly separated" — was
correct. `MilContext.Fresh()` clones per-team state, and it was **blanking
static lookup tables** instead of copying them. The FIRST team to take a context
kept the real contents; every team after it got nulls, zeros or an empty set.
Which team wins that race depends on tick and Think ordering, so the damage
moved from round to round. That is why it looked intermittent, and why bisecting
commits kept accusing whichever change happened to shift the ordering.

Two instances, both fixed:

| commit | fault |
|---|---|
| `bf38936` | `Array.CreateInstance(...)` gave a new EMPTY array; now `Array.Copy` |
| `90b0e34` | `NewCollection` kept only the comparer; now copies contents |

The proof was `'Barracks'` comparing not-equal to `"Barracks"`:

    [Centauri] option 3: 'Barracks' isStructure=True producesCombat=False

impossible unless `ProductionV3.HUMAN_PRODUCERS` is empty.

**Tables that were being corrupted:** `ProductionV3.HUMAN_PRODUCERS`,
`ProductionV3.WorkerNames`, `SpirePlanner.SPIRES`/`FOB_SPIRES`/`HUMAN_DEFENCE`/
`HUMAN_FOB_DEFENCE`, `ScoutPlanner.RING_FRACTIONS`/`ALIEN_SCOUTS`/`HUMAN_SCOUTS`.

**Every symptom DrMuck reported came from this one flaw:**
- Centauri built no factories -> `HUMAN_PRODUCERS` blanked
- Sol and Cent hoarded 80-90k -> one Barracks cannot spend a 75k budget
- Alien stopped building cysts/Cortex -> `RING_FRACTIONS` and `ALIEN_SCOUTS`
  blanked -> no scouting -> Bio Caches are fog-gated -> 185 shrimps not 565 ->
  `CountShrimps < FIRE_SHRIMPS` so no Quantum Cortex -> units UnmetPrerequisite
  -> `_busy=0` -> `PlaceProducers`' "idle producers are not the bottleneck"
  guard blocks every producer for the rest of the round
- Alien weak on close spawns -> `SPIRES` blanked -> no defence structures

**Verified after the fix** (3-way, all factions under the mod):
producer types Sol 5 / Cent 5 / Alien 3 (was 5/0/3); Cortex built; 7 Spires;
Cent fielding Assault Car, Combat Tank, Flak Car, Heavy Tank instead of only
Juggernauts; Sol with Light/Heavy/Ultra Heavy/Air factories; idle cash down from
80-90k to 9-31k; scouting per team, one side at 90.1% explored.

**Rule for this codebase:** any static field in a MilContext-swapped class is
per-team state. A constant lookup table must be `const`, or `static readonly` in
a class that is NOT swapped, or listed in `MilContext.KNOBS`.

WHAT THE FIX BOUGHT: factories are built every round (were zero), a Quantum
Cortex every round, 52-57 Spires, and the human unit mix is varied. The HQ
demand signal is now dominant, and `no valid spot within` dropped from 3-5 per
round to 1 across all three - the demand-sourced targets really are more
placeable.

WHAT IS STILL BROKEN, and it is the same thing as before the fix: **one HQ per
round**. Refineries are gated to ~600 m of an HQ, so income stays ~6x below the
alien's. HQ refusals across the three rounds:

    42 prerequisite (needs tier 4)   39 cash   24 reserve holds (<4000 eff)
     5 landing drifted                1 no valid spot

So expansion now loses a three-way race: the tier gate blocks it early, and once
the humans finally CAN spend (which the producer fix enabled) the money goes to
factories and units and never reaches the ~9,800 an HQ costs. This is where
DrMuck's "HQ expansion like expansion in a board game" idea belongs - it wants a
reservation or a priority, not another placement heuristic.

ALSO NEW: the alien now ends rounds on 228k-318k unspent cash at ~300/s income.
It out-earns everyone and cannot spend it.
